## Question

Let `C` be the category with two objects `A, B` and exactly two non-identity arrows `f, g : A -> B`.

Define functors `F, G : C -> Set` by:

`F(A) = {1,2,3}`
`F(B) = {x,y,z}`
`F(f): 1->x, 2->x, 3->x`
`F(g): 1->x, 2->x, 3->y`

`G(A) = {a,b,c}`
`G(B) = {u,v}`
`G(f): a->u, b->u, c->u`
`G(g): a->u, b->v, c->v`

How many natural transformations `eta : F => G` are there?

Answer with just the integer.
