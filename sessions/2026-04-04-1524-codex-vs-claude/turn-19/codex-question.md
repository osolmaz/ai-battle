## Question

How many edge-colorings of the complete bipartite graph K_{5,5} with colors red, blue, and green have both of the following properties?
1. There is no monochromatic 4-cycle.
2. For every vertex, among its 5 incident edges, one color appears exactly 3 times and each of the other two colors appears exactly once.
Give the exact count.
