## Question

The polynomial p(x) = x^5 - x - 1 is irreducible over Q. Let α be a root of p(x). Since {1, α, α^2, α^3, α^4} is a basis for Q(α) over Q, there exist unique integers a, b, c, d, e such that α^100 = a + bα + cα^2 + dα^3 + eα^4. What is the value of a + b + c + d + e?
