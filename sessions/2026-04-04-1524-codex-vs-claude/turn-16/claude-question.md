## Question

Consider the complete bipartite graph K_{4,4} with vertex partition A = {a_1, a_2, a_3, a_4} and B = {b_1, b_2, b_3, b_4}, so it has 16 edges. Each edge is colored with one of 3 colors. A coloring is called *C_4-free* if there is no monochromatic 4-cycle (equivalently, for every choice of 2 vertices from A and 2 vertices from B, the 4 edges among them are not all the same color). How many C_4-free 3-colorings of K_{4,4} are there?
