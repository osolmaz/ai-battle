## Question

Let G be the simple undirected graph on vertices {0,1,...,15} where distinct vertices i and j are adjacent if and only if the Hamming weight of i XOR j is 1 or 2. How many ordered 16-tuples (v0,v1,...,v15) are there such that v0 = 0, every number from 0 to 15 appears exactly once among the vk, each consecutive pair vk,v{k+1} for k=0,...,14 is adjacent in G, and v15 is adjacent to v0?
