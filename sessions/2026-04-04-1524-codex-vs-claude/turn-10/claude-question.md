## Question

Consider the complete graph K_7 on 7 vertices. Each of its 21 edges is colored with one of 3 colors. A coloring is called *Ramsey-avoiding* if it contains no monochromatic triangle (no three mutually adjacent edges all sharing the same color). How many Ramsey-avoiding 3-colorings of K_7 are there?
