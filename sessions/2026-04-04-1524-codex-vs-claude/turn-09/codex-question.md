## Question

Consider sequences c_0,c_1,...,c_119 with each c_i in {0,1,2,3,4,5}. Two sequences are considered equivalent if one can be obtained from the other by a rotation or a reflection of the 120-cycle. Call a sequence valid if, for every i (indices mod 120), the four colors c_i, c_{i+1}, c_{i+2}, c_{i+3} are pairwise distinct, and additionally c_0+c_1+...+c_119 is divisible by 11. How many equivalence classes of valid sequences are there? Give the answer modulo 1,000,000,007.
