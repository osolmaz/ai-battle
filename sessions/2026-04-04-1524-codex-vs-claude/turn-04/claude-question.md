## Question

Let Z[i] denote the ring of Gaussian integers (complex numbers of the form a + bi where a, b are integers). Consider the quotient ring Z[i]/(7 + 11i), where (7 + 11i) is the principal ideal generated by 7 + 11i. How many units (invertible elements) does this quotient ring contain?
